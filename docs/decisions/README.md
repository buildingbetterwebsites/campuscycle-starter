# Decision records

A decision record explains one choice your project made, so that a teammate or the next owner knows
why things are the way they are, and how to change them. A record is about half a page.

## How to write one

- **One decision per file**, in this folder, named after the decision: for example `analytics.md`.
- Write it when you decide, while you still know the reasons.
- Use these seven headings, each with a few sentences:
  1. **The question:** one sentence on what had to be decided, with the need or the requirement
     behind it.
  2. **Options considered:** at least two, each with what it would give and what it would cost. One
     of them is the simplest thing, or doing nothing, where that is a real option.
  3. **The decision:** which option you chose; once it is installed, what you installed, where its
     settings are, and how you checked it.
  4. **Why:** the reasons, each with its evidence, and what that evidence cannot tell you.
  5. **Consequences:** what you accepted with the choice: its cost in money and in time, who
     maintains it, what you give up, and how you could leave the tool later.
  6. **Facts read on:** the date you read each provider's pages, with their links.
  7. **Review when:** the event that should make someone look at the decision again.
- Decided as a team? Name who took part. Working alone? Your own reasons are enough.
- Keep every old record. When a decision changes, write a new record and say in the old one which
  record replaces it.

Your project needs **at least two decision records**. Two are ready for you to fill in, as a start:
[analytics.md](analytics.md) and [integration.md](integration.md). You may rename them or add more.
Under each heading, those two files have a box marked Tip with guidance, the options and an example.
The course's checks do not read those boxes, and you may delete them when you are done. For a record
of your own, add a file with the same seven headings and write your text under each one.

The course's final check reads every `.md` file in this folder except this README: at least two of
them must be filled in, with nothing left marked TODO, whatever their names.
