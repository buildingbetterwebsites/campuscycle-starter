# Where the starter's images come from

All six photos come from Wikimedia Commons. None shows a person. Each was resized to 1200 px wide (a JPEG, quality 78); nothing else was changed. When you use one, keep its line here: some licences require that you name the photographer and the licence.

Each photo was resized from the original and is shared under its own licence, as listed. The site's about page says the same, under "Photo credits", with each photo linked to its Commons page and each licence to its text (the seed writes it: `PHOTO_CREDITS` in `src/seed/seed.ts`).

When you replace them with images for your own site, use photos you are allowed to use (your own, or ones with an open licence) and record them here the same way. Never upload photos of people to this site.
